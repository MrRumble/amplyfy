About the band

Built to fit your event, not the other way round

AMPLFY is a live cover band built around one idea: your event, your way. We scale from a stripped-back acoustic duo for your ceremony and drinks reception to a full four-piece party band that fills the dancefloor all night.

Our set spans decades of the songs people actually want to hear — soulful, sing-along acoustic numbers for the early hours, and floor-filling classics once the party gets going. Every lineup is rehearsed tight and dressed to match the occasion.
